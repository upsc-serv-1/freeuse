## Question (Year: 2024 | Paper: GS IV)

3 (b) "Faith is of no avail in the absence of strength. Faith and strength, both are essential to accomplish any great work." Sardar Patel.

### Answer

| **Core Demand of the Question**
●        Explain the essence of the quote “Faith is of no avail in the absence of strength. Faith and strength, both are essential to accomplish any great work."
●        Examine the relevance of the quote in the present context. |
| --- |
**Answer**


**Vallabhbhai Patel**, a towering figure in India’s struggle for independence, remarked**faith**and**strength**as essential elements for achieving**greatness**.**Faith**provides the**motivation**,**purpose**, and**perseverance**needed to overcome challenges. It's the belief that something is possible, even when circumstances seem daunting.**Strength**is the physical, mental, and emotional capacity to act on that belief. It's the ability to work hard, endure hardships, and make sacrifices.


**Essence of the Quote:**


-**Philosophical Perspective:**Philosophically, faith embodies a**vision of possibility**and**belief**in higher truths and strength materialises the ability to actualise that vision through**action**and**perseverance**.

-**Psychological Insight:**Psychologically, faith serves as a**powerful motivator**, providing individuals with purpose and direction whereas strength gives the**capacity**to endure**challenges**and**setbacks**while maintaining resolve.

-**For example: Mahatma Jyotiba Phule**and**Savitribai Phule’s**belief in education as a means of empowerment inspired them to overcome challenges and**uplift marginalised communities**.

-**Ethical Consideration:**Faith guides individuals in adhering to**moral principles**and**values**, whereas strength lies in maintaining**integrity**and**moral courage**, even in the face of adversity.

-**For example: Eleanor Roosevelt’s**life exemplifies how faith in ethical principles and strength to maintain integrity made him a historical figure.

-**Leadership implication:**In leadership,**faith**inspires others by articulating a**compelling vision**and fostering belief in a shared mission whereas strength manifests in the ability to take**decisive action,**navigate obstacles, and lead by example.

-**For example: Satya Nadella’s**faith in his vision for**cloud computing**along with his strength to take decisive actions in restructuring and focusing on innovation made him an exemplary leader.

-**Personal development:**For personal development, faith encourages individuals to**transcend limitations**and embrace growth opportunities whereas**personal strength**involves**cultivating resilience, confidence**, and**self-efficacy**to overcome obstacles and achieve aspirations.

-**Resilience in Adversity**: Faith gives hope during adversity, while strength ensures perseverance through challenges.

-**For example**: India’s**COVID-19 vaccination drive**relied on public faith in health measures and the strength of its healthcare infrastructure.


** **


**Relevance of the Quote in the Present Context:**


-**Economic Self-sufficiency Initiatives:**Faith in India's economic potential, combined with strength in industrial policies, is key to achieving self-reliance.

-**For example**: The**Atma Nirbhar Bharatmission**relies on both faith in Indian innovation and the strength of its manufacturing sector.

-**National Integration and Social Harmony:**Faith in unity and the strength to address**social disparities**are essential for fostering harmony.

-**For example**: National integration efforts post-independence combined**faith**in a unified India with strong legal and social measures.

-**Sustainable Environmental Practices:**Faith in environmental sustainability must be backed by strong policies to mitigate climate change.

-**For example**: The**International Solar Alliance**showcases India’s faith in renewable energy, supported by the strength of its solar projects.

-**Public Health Infrastructure:**To ensure accessible healthcare for all, faith in universal healthcare needs to be supported by a strong health infrastructure.

-**For example**: The**Ayushman Bharat**initiative is built on faith in health equity and the strength of a robust healthcare delivery system.

-**Educational Reforms for Global Competitiveness**: Faith in education as a cornerstone of national progress requires the strength of effective reforms.


Sardar Patel’s philosophy highlights the need for a**balanced approach**, where both**faith**and**strength**are essential to achieving great success. In today’s rapidly changing world, this principle guides individuals and nations alike toward**sustainable development**,**unity**, and**resilience**, ensuring that belief is matched with action to create lasting progress.
